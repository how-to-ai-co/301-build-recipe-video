The rule

Every edit serves one ICP, solves one of their problems, and sits in one pillar. If a video can't name all three, it doesn't ship.

Purpose

Anyone can build anything now. So build a company.

Small companies will take over big ones through distribution, taste, design, style, a better and more specific product, and speed. A solo founder helping other people become solo founders, or level up their careers with AI if a company isn't their move yet.

Not consulting. A product, any product. Follow me if you want to build a company. Let me help you build your own. The philosophy stays, but the content goes down the stack: the actual steps of building a company.

Worldview

  • Building beats credentials. What you ship counts. What you hold doesn't.
  • Software got cheap; taste and distribution didn't. Making the thing is no longer the hard part.
  • Small beats big. One founder with AI outruns a department.
  • Institutions are slower and weaker than they look. Corporate, hiring, education.

Why listen

  • Several startups launched, from this account.
  • Corporate history: knows how the big side works from the inside.
  • A builder who ships, not a commentator.
  • Analytics background: can show the real numbers.
  • An audience already, which most builders don't have.

Why people relate

  • Not naturally good at marketing.
  • Not naturally good at design.
  • Letting go of things too early instead of holding on.
  • Constant failures, said out loud.
  • The builder trap: feature creep and growing scope instead of going after first customers.
  • Right now: a holding pattern, looking for revenue and seed, in public.

These aren't confessions. Each one is a mistake the audience is about to make.

Enemy and allies

The gatekeepers

  • Ladders and credential mills: certificates, courses that teach watching instead of building.
  • Corporate gatekeepers: hiring filters, permission culture, slowness.
  • Big AI labs pushing regulatory capture. Use their tools as hard as possible; don't trust their gatekeeping.

The builders, and the people locked out

Solo founders, vibepreneurs, operators, analysts, marketing managers, single moms, and kids graduating into a job market they think AI killed. Everyone who wants to level up and is being blocked out.

  • Larger type, stronger weights, fewer words for phone viewing.
  • Keep one pairing within a scene; switch only at deliberate section boundaries.
  • Keep series colors and category meanings stable through a piece.
  • Instagram 1080×1920: keep essentials inside x 35–980, y 220–1470.
  • The persistent title is the only added text unless Brian asks for more. No word cards or text baked into PNGs.

Charts and composition

  • Stable color mappings; pair color with names and line patterns (organic solid, paid dashed).
  • Faint grids, explicit units, readable labels, restrained fills.
  • Never mix dollars and counts on one scale. Tell zero apart from missing.
  • Orbitron for short chart titles only; Geist labels; Geist Mono summaries.
  • Thin borders, 10–12 px corners, little or no shadow. Gold used selectively, no metallic gradients.
  • Renaissance comes through hue and proportion, not ornamental frames or aged textures.
