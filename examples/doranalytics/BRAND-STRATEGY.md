# Doranalytics — brand strategy

Version 1 · October 19, 2026 · @doranalytics.xyz

Every edit we make serves **one ICP**, solves **one of their problems**, and
sits in **one pillar**. If a video can't name all three, it doesn't ship.

---

## 1. Purpose

**Anyone can build anything now. So build a company.**

Small companies will take over big ones through distribution, taste, design,
style, a better and more specific product, and speed. I'm a solo founder
helping other people become solo founders, or level up their careers with AI
if a company isn't their move yet.

Not consulting. A product, any product. *Follow me if you want to build a
company. Let me help you build your own.*

The philosophy stays, but the content goes **down the stack**: the actual
steps of building a company.

## 2. Perspectives (the worldview)

- **Building beats credentials.** What you ship counts. What you hold doesn't.
- **Software got cheap; taste and distribution didn't.** Making the thing is no longer the hard part.
- **Small beats big.** One founder with AI outruns a department.
- **Institutions are slower and weaker than they look.** Corporate, hiring, education.

## 3. Strengths (why listen)

- Several startups launched, from this account.
- Corporate history: I know how the big side works from the inside.
- A builder who ships, not a commentator.
- Analytics background: I can show the real numbers.
- An audience already, which most builders don't have.

## 4. Weaknesses (why people relate)

- Not naturally good at marketing.
- Not naturally good at design.
- Not holding on: letting go of things too early instead of having diamond hands.
- Constant failures, said out loud.
- The builder trap: feature creep and growing scope instead of going after
  first customers and new customers.
- Right now: a holding pattern, looking for revenue and seed, in public.

These aren't confessions. They're lessons people can use, and every one is a
mistake the audience is about to make.

## 5. Enemy: the gatekeepers

- Ladders and credential mills: certificates, courses that teach watching instead of building.
- Corporate gatekeepers: hiring filters, permission culture, slowness.
- **Big AI labs pushing regulatory capture.** The stance: use their tools as
  hard as possible (max plans, free tokens) and don't trust their gatekeeping.

## 6. Allies: the builders, and the people locked out

Solo founders, vibepreneurs, operators, analysts, marketing managers, single
moms, and kids graduating into a job market they think AI killed. Everyone
who wants to level up and is being blocked out.

## ICPs

| ICP | Their problem | What we show them |
|---|---|---|
| **Aspiring solo founder / vibepreneur** | Has ideas, doesn't know the steps from idea to revenue | How to build a company, step by step |
| **Career leveler** (analyst, marketing manager, operator) | Stuck on a ladder, afraid of being replaced | AI skills that make them the person who builds |
| **New grad** | Hates AI because they think the jobs are gone | You can create your own job |
| **Locked out** (single parents, career changers, no network) | Gatekeepers say no: no degree, no time, no connections | No permission needed; here's the path |

## Pillars

| # | Pillar | Built from | Share of posts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Build a Company**: idea, product, design, distribution, first customers, pricing, the whole stack | Purpose | 30% |
| 2 | **How to AI**: the tools and workflows; leveling up your career | Purpose + allies | 20% |
| 3 | **Founder in Public**: the raise, revenue, failures, the holding pattern | Weaknesses + strengths | 15% |
| 4 | **The Life**: Venice, coding on the beach, builder meetups, running, lifting. *This could be you.* | World building | 15% |
| 5 | **The New Rules**: small beats big, taste and distribution, where work is going | Perspectives | 10% |
| 6 | **No Gatekeepers**: credential mills, corporate permission, lab regulatory capture | Enemy | 10% |

Builders (allies) show up across all six as spotlights and collabs, not as a
pillar of their own.

## Formats by pillar

| Pillar | Formats |
|---|---|
| Build a Company | Step-by-step series ("Building a company, part N"), screen recording with captions, checklist carousels, "the one thing nobody tells you about X" talking head |
| How to AI | Tool walkthroughs on screen, "I built X in Y hours" timelapses, before and after a workflow, career-specific ("if you're an analyst, do this") |
| Founder in Public | Founder diary yap, real numbers on screen, postmortems of past startups, "the mistake I keep making" |
| The Life | B-roll montage with voiceover, coding-on-the-beach yap, run or lift with a voiceover thesis, meetup clips with builders |
| The New Rules | Talking-head takes, one-word-at-a-time typography for theses, carousels for frameworks |
| No Gatekeepers | Stunts and experiments (the 400-certificate bot), stitches and reactions, "they told you X, here's Y" |
| Builder spotlight (any pillar) | Duet or interview with a follower who shipped, "a single mom built this" story, build challenges |

## The edit check

Before an edit ships, write these three lines in its delivery notes:

```
ICP:     (one of the four)
Problem: (theirs, in their words)
Pillar:  (one of the six)
```
